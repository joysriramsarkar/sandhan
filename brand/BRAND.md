# সন্ধান (Sondhan) — Brand Guidelines

## 1. The Name
**সন্ধান** (Sondhan / Sandhan) — meaning *quest*, *search*, *inquiry*, and *discovery* in Bengali and Sanskrit.

## 2. Core Brand Philosophy
1. **Zero-Knowledge Privacy (শূন্য-জ্ঞান গোপনীয়তা)**: We do not track, log, or profile users. Encryption keys reside solely on the user's client device.
2. **Bengali-First Multilingual (বাংলা-প্রথম)**: World-class processing for Bengali with deep multilingual capabilities.
3. **Transparent Search (স্বচ্ছ অনুসন্ধান)**: Open algorithms, explainable ranking, and user-controlled Goggles.
4. **Radically Open (শতভাগ মুক্ত)**: Licensed under AGPL-3.0 to protect community ownership.

## 3. Color Palette
- **Forest Teal (`#0e7a63` / `#2fbf9a`)**: Represents trust, digital privacy, and calmness.
- **Warm Terracotta (`#e0762f` / `#f0954f`)**: Bengali cultural warmth, craft, and human vitality.
- **Parchment Light (`#f7f4ee`)** & **Obsidian Dark (`#0e1513`)**: Easy on the eyes for extended reading.
